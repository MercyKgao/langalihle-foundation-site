Who We Are

Langalihle Foundation NPO was established in 2021 to respond to the social, economic, and developmental challenges faced by rural and township communities in South Africa. Our approach is rooted in collaboration, sustainability, and long-term impact.

We believe that empowered communities are built through access to skills, infrastructure, culture, and opportunity — not handouts, but partnerships.

Vision

To create a world where every young person and disadvantaged community member has access to the resources, skills, and opportunities needed to thrive, live with dignity, and take pride in their culture and environment.

Mission

To empower and uplift youth and disadvantaged communities through skills development and sustainable community projects in infrastructure, housing, arts, culture, and environmental restoration, while fostering partnerships that enable long-term social and economic impact.

Core Values

Empowerment Community Development Compassion Collaboration Integrity Diversity & Inclusion Innovation Sustainability Excellence Passion

What We Do (Services)

We deliver practical services that improve daily living conditions and create pathways to self-reliance.

IT & Digital Skills

Bridging the digital divide through technology access and digital skills training (computer literacy, basic IT skills, cybersecurity awareness, and digital tools).

Electrical & Energy Support

Improving safety and access to reliable, sustainable energy solutions through basic repairs, safety upgrades, and energy-efficient support.

Plumbing & Water Services

Enhancing sanitation, hygiene, and water efficiency through repairs, installations, leak prevention, and water-saving solutions.

Arts & Culture Development

Preserving heritage and empowering creative expression through workshops, exhibitions, performances, and cultural events.

Farming & Food Security

Supporting sustainable agriculture through skills training, community gardens, irrigation support, and environmentally responsible practices.

Infrastructure Support

Practical upliftment initiatives that improve daily living conditions and strengthen community resilience.

Projects & Impact

Our flagship projects demonstrate what’s possible when communities and partners work together.

2024 – Community Solar Project (Tembisa)

Installed two solar power systems for disadvantaged households in informal settlements, providing reliable lighting and phone charging. Improved safety, communication, and overall quality of life.

2025 – Walk to Our Roots (Mashashane)

A 20km cultural and wellness initiative bringing together youth and elders on Good Friday, promoting health, reflection, unity, and cultural pride. A second event is planned for April.

Ongoing Programs

Ongoing IT support, infrastructure services, arts and culture programs, farming initiatives, and community development activities across rural and township communities.
